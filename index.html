Don't Wait for the Warning Label

Keeping the Human in the Lead in the Age of AI

Dr. Megan Alstot, Academy for Teaching and Learning, Baylor University
3rd Annual Protect Yourself! Conference, Better At Last, November 5, 2026

The big idea

AI amplifies your expertise. It doesn't replace your experience. AI can help you understand, but only humans should decide.

Five habits that keep the human in the lead

1

Prompt as a CARER

Give AI clear directions, the same way you would give them to a helper.

  • Context: what's going on and what you already know
  • Action: what you want it to do
  • Role: who it should act as
  • Example: how you want it (a list, a summary, one question at a time)
  • Rails: the limits (what to avoid, how long)
Fill in the bracketsContext: [What's going on and what I already know] Action: [What I want you to do] Role: Act as [who should answer, like a patient educator, a travel planner, or a holiday host] Example: Give me [the shape of the answer, like a short list, a summary, or one question at a time] Rails: [The limits, like what to avoid, how long, and plain language] Ask me any questions you need before you answer.
2

Manage the desk

One topic per chat, so important details don't slide off the workspace. In a long chat, ask for a summary, then paste it into a new chat.

Fill in the bracketsSummarize what we've discussed so far about [topic], including [the details I want to keep, like dates, limits, or decisions].
3

Lead with your expertise

AI amplifies human expertise. Start with a topic you know well, and watch where it's right, wrong, or overstated. AI can help you understand, only humans should decide!

Fill in the bracketsI know a lot about [a topic I know well]. Here is what I think: [my view or plan]. What are alternative points I may want to consider? What are other questions I can investigate? Give me two other perspectives on this issue.
4

Protect privacy

Don't give AI names or personal numbers (birthdate, Social Security, address, accounts, etc.). Keep it general: "someone in their 70s," "my bank," "a family member."

Fill in the bracketsMy [family member or friend, like granddaughter] is turning [age]. They love [interests] and live [general area, like out West]. Suggest [number] meaningful gift ideas, from something to buy to [something homemade or a care package].
5

Keep humans in your loop

Use AI to reach the people you love, with a birthday poem, a gift idea, or a conversation starter, and keep real connection human! Ask your grandkids how they use AI, and show them how you check things.

Fill in the bracketsWrite a [funny, kind, or heartfelt] [birthday poem, card message, or toast] for my [relationship, like best friend], who loves [interests]. Keep it to [length, like eight lines].

Have some fun

Kick the tires with something playful. No account is needed to try the free version of ChatGPT at chatgpt.com.

Fill in the bracketsWrite a [love song, ballad, opera, or limerick] from [a place or thing you love] to [another place or thing].
