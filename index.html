Five habits that keep the human in the lead

1

Prompt as a CARER

Give AI Context, an Action, a Role, an Example of how you want it, and Rails that set limits.

2

Manage the desk

One topic per chat so important details don't slide off the workspace.

3

Lead with your expertise

AI amplifies human expertise. AI can help you understand, only humans should decide!

4

Protect privacy

Don't give AI names or personal numbers (birthdate, Social Security, address, accounts, etc.).

5

Keep humans in your loop

Use AI to reach the people you love, with a birthday poem, a gift idea, or a conversation starter, and keep real connection human!
